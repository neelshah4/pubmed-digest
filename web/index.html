PubMed alert builder

One alert that covers everything you follow.

PubMed sends a separate email for every saved search, so following eight things means eight emails. This composes one query from the people and journals you pick, shows you how much mail to expect, then hands it to PubMed to send.

Step one

Follow specific people

How to enter a name Surname first with an initial is the most reliable: Surname A.
These also work, because the surname is identified from the results rather than from word order: Firstname Surname · Surname, A · Surname AB. Do not add a degree or a title. For a common surname, fill in the institution as well.

PubMed does not group authors, so one person can appear as several results when their affiliation changed or their middle initial comes and goes. Records that clearly belong together are combined already; tick the boxes to combine any that remain.

        Step four

        Hand it to PubMed

        PubMed sends the mail, not this site. Your address stays with your free My NCBI account, so you enter it once and never again.

        1. Open your query on PubMed using the button below.
        2. Sign in to My NCBI, or create a free account.
        3. Click Create alert under the search box, then choose weekly.

        Queries are built in your browser and sent straight to PubMed. Nothing is stored, no account is created here, and no email address is ever collected by this page.
